How to read this board

Implied value is a model, not a prediction: TVL × a category-family multiple (what first tokens of that kind of protocol have carried against TVL), adjusted by seven-day TVL momentum (clamped to −25%…+40%), by maturity (days live without a token, saturating) and by chain breadth. Protocols whose token already trades are counted in the header and never ranked. Wrapped, staked or bridged products of a trading token, versioned pools and centralised exchanges are set aside for the same reason: they are not launches waiting to happen. Farm plans are generated per protocol from its category, chains, TVL tier and age — costs are typical ranges, not quotes, and nothing here is financial advice.
